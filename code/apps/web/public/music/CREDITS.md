# Background music

All three tracks are original instrumental pieces composed for 1757 with AI
music generation (Google Lyria). They are mysterious, Bengal-court and
battlefield themes made for the Battle of Plassey setting.

| File | In-game name | Title | Mood |
|---|---|---|---|
| `court-of-murshidabad.mp3` | Court of Murshidabad | Whispers in the Palace | tanpura drone, sarangi, candlelit intrigue |
| `council-in-session.mp3` | Council in Session | The Council Divides | pulsing strings, bansuri, suspense |
| `eve-of-plassey.mp3` | Eve of Plassey | Storm over the Mango Grove | war drums, shehnai, monsoon storm |

To add a track, put the file here, add it to `TRACKS` in `apps/web/lib/music.ts`, and list it in this table.
